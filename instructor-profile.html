Biography & Teaching Philosophy

With over two decades of dedicated experience in education and a robust professional background in engineering, I offer a unique and comprehensive tutoring experience. Since 2001, I have been committed to helping students excel across a wide range of academic levels, from Year 7 through to A-Levels, specialising in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science. My long standing tenure in tutoring has allowed me to refine my teaching methods, ensuring that complex concepts are broken down into manageable and engaging lessons tailored to each student’s individual learning style.

By profession, I am a Computer Systems Engineer, which provides me with a practical, real-world perspective on the subjects I teach. I don’t just teach theory, I demonstrate how these principles are applied in the modern technology and engineering sectors, helping students understand the “why” behind their studies and inspiring them toward future career paths in STEM. My goal is to build confidence and foster a deep understanding of the subject matter, whether a student is looking to bridge foundational gaps or aiming for top marks in their A-Levels.

Throughout my career, I have developed a particular expertise and passion for working with students with special educational needs. I understand that every learner processes information differently, and I take pride in my ability to adapt my communication and teaching strategies to provide a supportive, patient, and inclusive environment. By building strong rapport and utilizing tailored pedagogical approaches, I have successfully helped many students with diverse learning requirements overcome academic hurdles and build the confidence necessary for long-term success.

We are always seeking talented and motivated individuals to join our team.
