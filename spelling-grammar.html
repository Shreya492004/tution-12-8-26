Spelling and Grammar

Spelling & Grammar Course Overview

Spelling and Grammar form the foundation of every successful English learner. Strong literacy skills enable students to communicate clearly, write accurately, and perform confidently across all areas of the curriculum. Our comprehensive programme helps students develop a solid understanding of English grammar while building an extensive vocabulary and improving spelling accuracy.

Through engaging lessons, structured practice, and regular assessments, students strengthen their language skills, improve written communication, and gain the confidence required for classroom success, 11+ examinations, Grammar School entrance tests, Independent School assessments, and future academic studies.

1. Building Strong Grammar Foundations

Students develop a thorough understanding of English grammar, including parts of speech, sentence structure, punctuation, verb tenses, clauses, and agreement. These essential skills form the basis of clear and effective communication.

2. Spelling Rules & Vocabulary Development

Learn proven spelling techniques while expanding vocabulary through structured word study. Students gain confidence in recognising spelling patterns, prefixes, suffixes, root words, and commonly confused words used in everyday and academic English.

3. Sentence Construction & Accuracy

Students learn how to construct clear, grammatically correct sentences using varied sentence structures. Lessons focus on improving fluency, coherence, punctuation, and overall writing accuracy.

4. Editing & Proofreading Skills

Develop the ability to identify and correct spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors. Students learn effective proofreading strategies that improve the quality and accuracy of their written work before submission or examination.

5. Grammar for Examination Success

Strengthen grammar and spelling skills specifically for 11+, SATs, Grammar School, Independent School, and GCSE examinations. Students become familiar with common assessment styles and develop the confidence to answer language questions accurately.

6. Applying Grammar in Writing

Students learn how to apply grammar rules naturally within creative, descriptive, persuasive, and formal writing. Emphasis is placed on producing clear, engaging, and well-structured pieces that demonstrate strong language skills.

7. Confidence Through Regular Practice

Through interactive exercises, quizzes, revision activities, and personalised feedback, students continually reinforce their learning. By the end of the course, they will have developed the confidence and language skills needed to communicate effectively and achieve success in both examinations and everyday English.
