Helping Students Build a Brighter Future

London Star Education was founded in 2020, at the start of the Covid-19 pandemic, with one goal in mind: to provide online study methods that enable as many students as possible to achieve academic excellence.

We prepare students to sit the 11+/13+ entrance exams and interviews. Our trusted learning techniques, along with our skilled, experienced Oxford-educated teachers, have ensured the success of hundreds of students.

Our students have received offers from top ranked schools including:

  • Queen Elisabeth School for Boys
  • Henrietta Barnett School
  • Eton College
  • Westminster School
  • St Paul's Girls' School

In addition, we organise in-person summer camps. Contact us today so we can help you build your brighter future.

How We Work

A Simple Path to Academic Success

At London Star Education, we make online learning simple, engaging, and effective. From understanding each student's learning needs to delivering personalised lessons and continuous progress tracking, we support every learner in achieving their academic goals with confidence.
